Reconstruct

6-view carving · depth-assisted · re-projection scored
Objective: the reconstruction must re-project back to your original images. Every step is scored by IoU against your input silhouettes — if the score is low, the model is wrong regardless of how it looks.

Views

Silhouette

Larger pixels quantize the mask into blocks before carving, giving a chunky voxel look and a much faster carve. 1 px is the full-detail mask.

Alignment

Real photos never share exact scale/centering. Auto-align normalizes each silhouette's bounding box so the views actually correspond. Leave this on.
Bounding-box alignment is only a guess. This searches per-view scale and offset to maximise real IoU, then keeps the best fit found. It is the single biggest lever on your score.

Carving

Exact starts from the part every view agrees on, then adds the least geometry needed so no mask pixel is left unexplained. Missing goes to zero while extra stays far below a plain union.
The trusted starting volume. Keep at 6/6 — the patching pass fills in whatever that leaves out.

Depth assist

Monocular depth is relative, not metric — it says what is nearer, never by how much. So it is applied as a weak refinement on top of silhouette carving, never as the primary constraint. At influence 0 this is pure silhouette carving, which is the trustworthy baseline.
